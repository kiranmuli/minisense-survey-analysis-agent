GreenLeaf Bistro — Customer Experience FAQ

About Us
GreenLeaf Bistro is a farm-to-table cafe founded in 2019, serving fresh,
seasonal, and locally sourced meals. We operate three locations across the
city and pride ourselves on a warm, relaxed atmosphere where guests can dine
in, order at the counter, or book ahead through our mobile app. Our mission is
simple: honest food, friendly service, and a space that feels like a second
home.

Q: What are your most popular menu items?
A: Our top sellers are the Avocado Toast, the Garden Bowl, and our house Cold
Brew Coffee. Seasonal specials rotate monthly, and our kitchen prepares a fresh
vegan option every day. Ingredients are delivered each morning from partner
farms within 50 miles of the city.

Q: What is your average wait time?
A: We target under 10 minutes for counter orders during off-peak hours. Peak
hours (12–1 PM and 6–8 PM) may see 15–20 minute waits. During busy periods we
open additional registers and encourage guests to order ahead in the app to
skip the line. If your wait exceeds 25 minutes, please speak to a team member —
a free drink is offered as an apology.

Q: How do you handle complaints?
A: All complaints are escalated to the shift manager within 15 minutes.
Refunds or replacements are offered for any quality issue, no questions asked.
Written feedback from our post-visit surveys is reviewed weekly by store
managers, and recurring themes are shared with the operations team to drive
improvements.

Q: What is your CSAT target?
A: We aim for a Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) score of 4.5 or higher on our
1–5 scale. Any store scoring below 4.0 in a given week triggers a root-cause
review with the operations team, where we examine survey free-text comments to
identify what went wrong — most often wait times, food quality, or staffing.

Q: Do you take reservations or online bookings?
A: Yes. You can reserve a table or place a pickup order through the GreenLeaf
mobile app or our website. App users earn loyalty points on every order, which
can be redeemed for free drinks and menu items. If the app is unavailable,
walk-ins are always welcome.

Q: What are your opening hours?
A: All locations open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, including weekends and
most public holidays. Our breakfast menu is served until 11:00 AM, after which
the full lunch and dinner menu becomes available.

Q: How do you ensure cleanliness and food safety?
A: Every location follows a documented hourly cleaning checklist and holds the
top municipal food-hygiene rating. Tables are sanitized after each guest, and
all kitchen staff complete food-safety certification before their first shift.

Q: How can I share feedback?
A: After each visit we send a short survey by email or in the app asking for a
1–5 rating and an optional comment. We read every response. Your feedback
directly shapes our menu, staffing levels, and service improvements — thank you
for helping GreenLeaf Bistro get better every day.
